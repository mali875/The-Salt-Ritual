    BEFORE YOUR RITUAL

    The details.

    What’s included in the price?

    Your chosen size, scrub base, base oil, scent, skin booster and one botanical. A second botanical costs £2 per jar. You can also choose no skin booster or no botanical.

    Can I buy my scrub today?

    Online ordering is coming soon. You can create a blend and save it in your basket now, but payments are not yet available. Saving a basket does not place an order.

    What are the jar quantities?

    Small is intended for one use. Jar weights are being finalised and will be added before online ordering opens.

    What if I have ingredient allergies?

    Our menu includes sweet almond oil and other botanical ingredients. Check the finished product’s ingredient list before purchasing. Final ingredient and fragrance information will be available before orders open.

    Will my basket be saved?

    Your basket is saved in this browser when browser storage is available. It is not sent to us. You can download a copy of your blend choices from the basket.
